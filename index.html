Yuxiao Hu 胡愉霄

PhD Candidate

I am a PhD candidate at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, advised by Prof. Jinyue Yan and Associate Prof. Yuntian Chen. I completed my master's at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences under Prof. Chengxin Zhao.

My research focuses on automated scientific discovery, with current interests in symbolic regression and research agents powered by large language models. I am also interested in trustworthy and reproducible evaluation, building on my earlier work in adversarial robustness.
